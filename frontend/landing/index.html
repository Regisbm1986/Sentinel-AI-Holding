Vision

Sentinel OS aims to make cyber defense more autonomous, auditable and resilient by design.

Security that can act, not just alert

The platform is designed to turn signals into structured action: detect, prioritize, coordinate and execute with guardrails.

  • Autonomy with oversight Every decision path remains inspectable and reviewable.
  • Defense as a system Security, observability and operations work together instead of separately.
  • Built for extension New agents, policies and workflows can be added without redesigning the core.

What the platform should become

A dependable operating layer for autonomous cyber defense across teams, workloads and environments.

  • Operational clarity One control plane for signals, decisions and outcomes.
  • Technical trust Transparent architecture that welcomes review and contribution.
  • Incremental adoption Start small, validate quickly and scale with confidence.
